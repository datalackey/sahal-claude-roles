## DECISIONS.md maintenance

When making a significant architecture/design change under `devenv/claude/`
(e.g. changing what gets bind-mounted, how config sync works, security
boundaries, or anything future readers would ask "why is it done this way?"
about), add an entry to `devenv/claude/DECISIONS.md` capturing the *why*, not
just the *what*, before considering the change complete.
